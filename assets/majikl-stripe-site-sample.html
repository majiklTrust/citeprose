What we do

majiklTrust Market Intelligence is delivered as a hosted, multi-tenant software service. Each customer works inside an isolated workspace where credentials and data are separated at the database level. There is no physical product and nothing is shipped. Customers pay a recurring subscription for access to the following capabilities.

Market intelligence monitoring

The platform continuously monitors curated news and RSS sources relevant to your industry, sanitizing and screening incoming content before it is ever used.

AI content generation

It researches topics and drafts LinkedIn posts grounded in cited primary sources, with fidelity checks so that figures in a post match the values in the underlying source.

Human approval workflow

Role-based approval queues for owners, editors, and viewers mean nothing publishes without a person signing off. Every draft, edit, and approval is tracked.

Scheduling and history

A workspace dashboard holds drafts, scheduled posts, and full publishing history, so teams can plan ahead and review everything that has gone out.

LinkedIn publishing

Publish to LinkedIn organization pages and personal profiles from a single dashboard, with a deliberate exactly-once publishing design that avoids duplicate posts.

Analytics

Post performance metrics are pulled back from LinkedIn into a curated analytics view, so you can see how published content performed against your goals.

Employee advocacy

Team members connect their own LinkedIn profiles and receive personalized variants of company content in a self-scoped queue to approve, edit, or decline, with optional opt-in automation.

Organic to paid bridge

High-performing organic posts can be bridged into paid campaigns. The platform never spends autonomously; a person always creates and authorizes any paid campaign.

Security and tenant isolation

Strict multi-tenant isolation, encrypted credentials, and a fail-closed design keep each customer's data, tokens, and content separated and protected.

Refund and dispute policy

We want you to be satisfied with majiklTrust Market Intelligence. Because we sell access to a digital software service and not physical goods, the following refund and dispute terms apply.

Refund window

You may request a full refund of your most recent subscription payment within 14 days of that charge by emailing accountadmin@majikl.com from the email address on your account. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. After the 14-day window has passed, subscription payments are non-refundable, and you may cancel at any time to stop future renewals as described in the cancellation policy below.

How to request a refund

Send your workspace name and the date of the charge to accountadmin@majikl.com. We will confirm receipt and process eligible refunds promptly.

Disputes and chargebacks

If you believe you were charged in error or see a charge you do not recognize, please contact us first at accountadmin@majikl.com so we can resolve it quickly. We review every dispute in good faith and will correct genuine billing errors. Contacting us directly is usually faster than filing a payment dispute, and it allows us to investigate the specific charge on your account.

Cancellation policy

majiklTrust Market Intelligence is sold as a recurring monthly subscription. The following terms govern cancellation.

Cancel at any time

You may cancel your subscription at any time by emailing accountadmin@majikl.com from the email address on your account. There is no cancellation fee and no long-term commitment beyond the current billing period.

What happens when you cancel

When you cancel, your subscription remains active through the end of the billing period you have already paid for, and it will not renew after that. You keep access to your workspace until the paid period ends. You will not be billed again once the cancellation takes effect. Refunds for the current period, if any, are governed by the refund policy above.

Non-renewal

Subscriptions renew automatically each month until you cancel. Cancelling stops the automatic renewal so that no further charges are made to your payment method.
