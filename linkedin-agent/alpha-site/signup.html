Get started

Set up your workspace in three steps.

Create your login, name your workspace, pick your plan. You are publishing-ready in minutes.

  • 1.Create your login and verify your email Create your account with our Identity & Authroization team, then follow the verification link in the email you will receive. Verification is required: your workspace can only be created for an email address you have proven is yours.
    Create Your Account
  • 2.Name your workspace After you verify and sign in, the dashboard offers one button: Set Up My Workspace. It walks you through naming your workspace and connecting your AI model key. That key is yours, is encrypted at rest, and powers all content generation for your workspace.
  • 3.Pick your plan With the workspace created, the dashboard takes you to the billing page to choose a plan. Your subscription activates at checkout and your workspace unlocks immediately.

What you will need

An email address you can verify, and an AI model provider API key for language generation. Anthropic keys are supported today; you can create one at console.anthropic.com. Image generation providers can be added later from your workspace's management page.

Invited by your organization?

If an administrator invited you, use the link in your invitation email instead; it carries your workspace assignment. If you signed in and see "No Membership", your invitation may be waiting: log out and back in after verifying your email, and it will be accepted automatically.
