4. LinkedIn Integration and Data Use
Our Service integrates with the LinkedIn Community Management API. This section describes specifically how we handle LinkedIn member and organization data in compliance with LinkedIn's API Terms of Service.

4a. Data We Access from LinkedIn
When you connect your LinkedIn organization page to our Service, we may access, consistent with the permissions you grant:

Organization page identity (name, logo, page URN);
Organization page posts, comments, and reactions;
Basic profile information of members who interact with your page (name, headline, public profile URL), as returned by LinkedIn's API;
Page follower counts and engagement analytics; and
OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens required to perform actions on your behalf.
4b. How We Use LinkedIn Data
To publish content to your connected LinkedIn organization page at your explicit direction;
To display engagement data (comments, reactions, analytics) within your dashboard; and
To maintain authentication tokens necessary to maintain your connected session.
4c. Restrictions on LinkedIn Data
We do not sell LinkedIn member data to third parties;
We do not use LinkedIn member data for advertising or retargeting;
We do not combine LinkedIn member data with data from other sources to build profiles of individual members;
We do not store LinkedIn member personal data beyond the period required to render the Service features you have requested; and
We comply with LinkedIn's data storage and deletion requirements, including removing LinkedIn data upon account disconnection or termination.
4d. Revoking LinkedIn Access
You may disconnect your LinkedIn account or organization page from within the Service settings at any time, or by revoking access directly through your LinkedIn account settings at linkedin.com/psettings/permitted-services. Upon disconnection, we will delete stored LinkedIn OAuth tokens promptly.
