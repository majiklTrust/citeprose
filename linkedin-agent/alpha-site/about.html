Brandon, Founder, majiklTrust Market Intelligence, LLC. Illinois, USA.

majiklTrust is a one person company, and that is a design decision, not a limitation. The founder who set the standard is the engineer who enforces it, on every line of the platform, from the database to the page you are reading now.

The story

Brandon has spent his career in the space where software meets trust: building systems where being wrong is expensive and being unverifiable is worse. Somewhere along the way a professional habit hardened into a personal rule: a claim is worth exactly as much as the trail behind it. Confident statements get checked against the source. Every time. Even his own, and especially the ones that sound most certain.

Market Intelligence is that rule, written in code. He watched AI tools learn to write faster than they learned to be right, and watched professionals pay for it with the only currency that matters on a professional network: credibility. So he built the tool he was willing to put his own name behind. It reads continuously from sources you choose, corroborates before it drafts, cites what it read, and then stops. It does not publish. You do. The platform's job ends where your judgment begins, and that boundary is load bearing.

He builds the whole thing himself: the research pipeline, the drafting engine, the encryption, the review queue, the infrastructure it all runs on. Not because help is unwelcome, but because accountability should have a name on it. When something is wrong, you are talking to the person who can fix it, usually the same day.

The principles the platform is held to

  • The human has the first, last, and final word. No post reaches your page without your explicit approval. Brand safety is a step you perform, not a setting you hope is on.
  • Verify first, always. Nothing is drafted from thin air. Research is grounded in the sources you trust and corroborated across independent reporting before a word is written.
  • Fail closed. When the platform is uncertain, it stops and says so. Silence and pretending are failure modes here, treated exactly as seriously as crashes.
  • Earn trust, do not assert it. The name is a promise with a working definition: trust that can be traced, checked, and re earned on every single post.
