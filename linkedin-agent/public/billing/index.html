What your subscription covers

This workspace is an isolated tenant of a hosted, multi-tenant software service. Credentials and data are separated at the database level. Your subscription pays for access to the following capabilities.

Market intelligence monitoring

The platform continuously monitors curated news and RSS sources relevant to your industry, sanitizing and screening incoming content before it is ever used.

AI content generation

It researches topics and drafts posts grounded in cited primary sources, with fidelity checks so that figures in a post match the values in the underlying source.

Human approval workflow

Role-based approval queues for owners, editors, and viewers mean nothing publishes without a person signing off. Every draft, edit, and approval is tracked.

AI Vendor

The language model behind content generation. Keys are validated with the vendor before anything is stored, and stored encrypted per workspace. This platform never purchases vendor service on your behalf.
