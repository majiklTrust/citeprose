2. Information We Collect

2a. Information You Provide

  • Account registration: Business name, contact name, business email address, organization website, and billing information.
  • Platform configuration: Content topics, RSS feed subscriptions, publishing preferences, and API credentials you supply (such as your Anthropic API key).
  • Communications: Any messages you send to us via email or support channels.

2b. Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage data: Pages visited, features accessed, actions taken within the platform, timestamps, and session duration.
  • Device and log data: IP address, browser type, operating system, referral URL, and server log data.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: See Section 9 for details.

2c. Information from Third Parties

  • LinkedIn: When you connect your LinkedIn account or organization page, we receive OAuth tokens and, subject to the permissions you grant, organization page data, post engagement data, and basic profile information as described in Section 4.
  • Identity providers: If you authenticate via Auth0, WorkOS, or a similar identity provider, we receive authentication tokens and the profile attributes those services share.
  • RSS and web content feeds: We collect publicly available content from third-party news and media sources you configure for research purposes.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provision, operate, and improve the Service;
  • Authenticate users and manage tenant accounts;
  • Generate AI-assisted research summaries and draft social media content on your behalf;
  • Publish content to LinkedIn or other platforms at your direction and with your authorization;
  • Process payments and maintain billing records;
  • Respond to support requests and communicate service updates;
  • Monitor system performance, detect errors, and ensure platform security;
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations; and
  • Enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not use your data to train external AI models, sell your data to advertisers, or engage in behavioral advertising.

4. LinkedIn Integration and Data Use

Our Service integrates with the LinkedIn Community Management API. This section describes specifically how we handle LinkedIn member and organization data in compliance with LinkedIn's API Terms of Service.

4a. Data We Access from LinkedIn

When you connect your LinkedIn organization page to our Service, we may access, consistent with the permissions you grant:

  • Organization page identity (name, logo, page URN);
  • Organization page posts, comments, and reactions;
  • Basic profile information of members who interact with your page (name, headline, public profile URL), as returned by LinkedIn's API;
  • Page follower counts and engagement analytics; and
  • OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens required to perform actions on your behalf.

4b. How We Use LinkedIn Data

  • To publish content to your connected LinkedIn organization page at your explicit direction;
  • To display engagement data (comments, reactions, analytics) within your dashboard; and
  • To maintain authentication tokens necessary to maintain your connected session.

4c. Restrictions on LinkedIn Data

  • We do not sell LinkedIn member data to third parties;
  • We do not use LinkedIn member data for advertising or retargeting;
  • We do not combine LinkedIn member data with data from other sources to build profiles of individual members;
  • We do not store LinkedIn member personal data beyond the period required to render the Service features you have requested; and
  • We comply with LinkedIn's data storage and deletion requirements, including removing LinkedIn data upon account disconnection or termination.

4d. Revoking LinkedIn Access

You may disconnect your LinkedIn account or organization page from within the Service settings at any time, or by revoking access directly through your LinkedIn account settings at linkedin.com/psettings/permitted-services. Upon disconnection, we will delete stored LinkedIn OAuth tokens promptly.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share information in the following limited circumstances:

We engage vetted third-party service providers who process data on our behalf, including cloud hosting providers (AWS), authentication services (Auth0, WorkOS), payment processors, and email delivery services. 6. Data Retention

We retain account and usage data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service, plus a reasonable period thereafter to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

LinkedIn-sourced data is retained only for as long as necessary to render the dashboard features you have requested. Upon account cancellation or LinkedIn disconnection, LinkedIn OAuth tokens and cached member data are deleted within 30 days.

Billing records are retained for a minimum of seven (7) years as required by Illinois law and standard accounting practices.
